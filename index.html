    How to play

    1. Read the course name. Every day there are five famous golf courses, the same for every player.
    2. Spin and zoom the globe, then tap where you think the course is. Your first tap is your guess, so spin first and tap once. Prefer a confirm step? Turn on Confirm each guess in Settings.
    3. Watch the flag drop on the real spot and read a fact about the course. The closer your ball, the lower your score: from 50 under par for a ball on the pin to 50 over for one on the far side of the world. The next hole tees off a few seconds later; touch the globe to stay and look around.
    4. The back tees count more. Holes 1–2 play from the white tees (×1), hole 3 from the blue tees (×2), and holes 4–5 from the tips (×3), multiplying every stroke. A round runs from −500 to +500. Lowest score wins.

    Scoring

    Land in the right country and the hole is never worse than a bogey. A new set of courses tees off at midnight, your local time.
