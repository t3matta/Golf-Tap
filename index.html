    How to play

    1. Read the course. Every day there are five famous golf courses, the same for every player.
    2. Spin and zoom the globe, then tap where you think the course is. Tap again to move your ball.
    3. Lock it in. The flag drops on the real spot. The closer your ball, the more points, up to 100.
    4. The back tees count more. Holes 1–2 play from the white tees (×1), hole 3 from the blue tees (×2), and holes 4–5 from the tips (×3). A perfect round is 1,000.
